about me

Hello! My name is Megan (she/her), and I'm a Product Manager at Xbox, where I focus on crafting discovery experiences that connect players with the games they love.

I specialize in building AI-driven metadata systems that power personalized discovery, helping players find what they love while enabling publishers to reach the right audiences.

Outside of work, I'm both a gamer and a game dev, and I bring that same creativity and player-first mindset into everything I build, advocating for experiences that engage, include, and inspire.
