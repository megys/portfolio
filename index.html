Product Manager — Metadata, Wishlist & PC Catalog

Jan 2024 – Mar 2026

Owned the metadata, storefront, and PC catalog experiences that help players find and buy games — building the AI data layer behind Xbox's recommendations, shipping the discovery and commerce features on top of it, and expanding what's available to play on PC.

Discovery & AI metadata
  • Took the Gaming Metadata system — Xbox's AI game-tagging platform, the behind-the-scenes data that powers recommendations, search, and personalization — from an early foundation to a live service, rebuilding tagging quality with a retrieval-augmented (RAG) pipeline and reprocessing the entire ~15,000+ title catalog.
  • Shipped Browse by Mood on PC Home — the first player-facing feature built on that metadata — letting players discover games by the vibe they're in the mood for (cozy, competitive, horror) instead of hunting through genres. In an A/B test across ~7.4M players it drove a statistically significant lift in players clicking through to install games, and became a reusable template for future channels.
  • Added human-in-the-loop guardrails so people can review and correct the AI's tags — scaling the system responsibly without letting bad data reach players.
Commerce & monetization
  • Shipped Deals for You, a personalized channel that surfaces deals on games each player actually cares about — driving a +9.4% increase in Game Pass sign-ups.
  • Shipped the Wishlist channel permanently on the PC Store, putting the games players saved for later right in front of them when they're ready to buy — lifting wishlist views +9% and wishlist actions +12%.
  • Became product owner for the Benefits Hub and shipped third-party subscription compliance (Fortnite Crew, Ubisoft+ Classics, EA Play) across Console, PC, and Mobile on a tight timeline.
Catalog & content quality
  • Built the Quality Control filter from the ground up — a brand-new system that keeps low-quality games from taking up space on the storefront, so players mostly see games worth their time. Took it from a blank page to a shipped, always-on feature live on both PC and Console, after proving it lifted engagement without hurting sales.
  • Owned the Gaming Metadata system end-to-end and expanded the Xbox PC catalog by adding hundreds of previously missing games — resolving a long-standing player request that earned coverage in The Verge and PC Gamer, and contributing to the Xbox Play Anywhere 1,000-titles milestone.
Research & strategy
  • Commissioned and socialized user research — including a "what makes an Xbox game?" study — and drove its findings into store-strategy and game-visibility decisions across teams.
