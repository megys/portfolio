Product Manager — Metadata, Wishlist & PC Catalog

Jan 2024 – Mar 2026

Owned the metadata, storefront, and PC catalog experiences that help players find and buy games — building the AI data layer behind Xbox's recommendations, shipping the discovery and commerce features on top of it, and expanding what's available to play on PC.

Discovery & AI metadata
  • Took the Gaming AI Metadata system — Xbox's AI game-tagging platform, the behind-the-scenes data that describes each game with tags to power recommendations, search, and personalization — from an early foundation to a live service, rebuilding tagging quality with a retrieval-augmented (RAG) pipeline and reprocessing the entire ~15,000+ title catalog.
  • Shipped Browse by Mood on PC Home — the first player-facing feature built on that metadata — letting players discover games by the vibe they're in the mood for (cozy, competitive, horror), giving players a more expressive way to browse beyond our traditional genre filters. In an A/B test across ~7.4M players it drove a statistically significant lift in players clicking through to install games, and became a reusable template for future channels.
  • Added human-in-the-loop guardrails so people can review and correct the AI's tags — scaling the system responsibly without letting bad data reach players.
Commerce & monetization
  • Shipped Deals for You, a personalized channel that surfaces deals on games each player actually cares about — driving a +9.4% increase in Game Pass sign-ups.
  • Shipped the permanent Wishlist channel on the PC Store, spotlighting saved games on sale or in Game Pass so players can act at the right moment — lifting wishlist views +9% and actions +12%.
  • Became product owner for the Benefits Hub, the centralized destination where players discover benefits included with Xbox and Game Pass membership, and enabled eligible Game Pass Ultimate members to access partner benefits from Fortnite Crew, Ubisoft+ Classics, and EA Play across Console, PC, and Mobile.
Catalog & content quality
  • Built the Quality Control filter from the ground up to protect players from storefront abuse while giving more visibility to developers who invest care in their games. Testing showed it lifted engagement without negatively impacting sales, validating the approach for production rollout across PC, Console, and Mobile.
  • Owned the Gaming AI Metadata system end-to-end and expanded the Xbox PC catalog by adding hundreds of previously missing games — resolving a long-standing player request that earned coverage in The Verge and PC Gamer, and contributing to the Xbox Play Anywhere 1,000-titles milestone.
Research & strategy
  • Commissioned and socialized user research — including a "what makes an Xbox game?" study — and drove its findings into store-strategy and game-visibility decisions across teams.
